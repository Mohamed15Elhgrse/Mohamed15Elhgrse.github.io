Tele-robotic stabilization for beating-heart surgery

PhD research · KFUPM, IRC for Bio Systems and Machines · Supervisor: Dr. Momen Abayazid

Operating on a beating heart means working on a target that never holds still. The system is built around two Franka arms. One carries a camera that tracks the heart and keeps the surgeon's view steady. The other holds the surgical tool and follows the surgeon's hand through a haptic device.

The first version is a one-way link. A Force Dimension Sigma.7 commands the end-effector of a Franka Research 3 under Cartesian impedance control, so the arm follows the hand and stays compliant. Force feedback to the hand is formulated and is the next step.

Earlier in the project I built a geometric SE(3) PD controller for the arm in ROS 2 and Gazebo. The wrist's very small rotational inertia made uniform gains unstable, so I moved to an operational-space formulation with joint-space damping.

Intelligent Systems and Control · Fall 2025 · with Fady Eskandar

Fuzzy–ANFIS formation control for swarms of ground robots

A fuzzy leader–follower controller gathers up to 40 robots into a ring around the leader and steers them past obstacles. An ANFIS model trained on the fuzzy controller's own data then replaces the hand-designed leader controller and reproduces it to within 4.5% of the output range, with no obstacle collisions in matched runs of 15 to 40 followers.

  • MATLAB
  • Fuzzy logic
  • ANFIS
  • Multi-robot

Optimal Control · Spring 2026 · with Tarek Eissa

Energy-optimal tilt scheduling for a quad-tilt-wing UAV

We reproduced the SDRE–LQR flight controller of Samadikhoshkho and Lipsett (Drones, 2025) in MATLAB, with six-degree-of-freedom nonlinear dynamics, an LQR outer loop for position and velocity and an SDRE inner loop for attitude. We then extended it with an energy-optimal schedule for the wing tilt angle across a vertical take-off, a 12 m/s cruise and a landing.

  • MATLAB
  • LQR
  • SDRE
  • UAV

System Identification · Fall 2025 · with Tarek Eissa

Noise-resilient ARX model of a quadcopter in hover

A black-box model of a four-degree-of-freedom quadcopter, identified from the open AscTec Pelican flight dataset. Multi-input single-output ARX structures are tuned with a genetic algorithm, then tested on flights the model has not seen, with measurement noise added at 30 dB SNR.

  • MATLAB
  • ARX
  • Genetic algorithm
  • System identification

Linear Multivariable Control · Fall 2025 · individual project

H∞, PID and pole-placement control of a quadcopter

I derived a nonlinear model of a four-degree-of-freedom quadcopter (roll, pitch, yaw and altitude), linearized it and designed three controllers on the same plant. Each one is tested against an input disturbance, measurement noise, and both together.

  • MATLAB
  • Simulink
  • H∞
  • Robust control
